I am a software engineer who spends quite a lot of his time software engineering.

This project was inspired by me playing modded minecraft one weekend.

It was so flow state inducing and kept my focus -- I did not feel overwhelmed at all, even though largely I was building tech, just like in software engineering.

ONE of the reasons I came to believe this is true, is spatial organization. Our brain remembers and contains things in spaces, however in software engineering, the portal to everything, at least the way I do it, is kind of like it's all just 'on your computer'. In other words, it's not like your cron jobs for project X live in X 45 and Z 37. They just live on your computer with everything else. When you work on Many Many projects like I do, and they get quite big, it's hard to create a mind map of everything, and it just feels cluttered.

So I was thinking, how could you spatially organize, similar to Minecraft. And that's where the idea for Atreides came. Basically we'll use @xyflow/react to create a graph (flow diagram) based organization system, where things are organized spatially and hierarchically.
